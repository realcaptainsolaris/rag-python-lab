# Billing period FAQ

## When is usage billed?

The billing cycle is a calendar month in UTC. Invoices are issued on day 1 of the following month in EUR. Usage is based on the daily peak registered devices. An observation count is not the billing device count, and a temporarily disconnected registered device still appears in the inventory.

To reconcile a monthly invoice, compare the daily inventory peaks with the organisation record. Summing the daily peaks would answer a different question from finding each day's peak. Keep the calculation basis visible in an internal report rather than labeling every quantity as usage.

## When does a plan change apply?

A plan change applies on the next UTC day. Downloadable invoice records remain available for 12 months. Changing a plan does not rewrite invoices already issued for an earlier period.

Record the effective date rather than relying only on the time an administrator clicked the change. A request just before midnight UTC can be displayed on a different local date. Local business hours do not change the billing boundary.

## What does this page cover?

This page explains period boundaries and usage attribution. The organisation billing contact handles account-specific questions. A plan capacity table describes device limits and retention, not a binding quotation for a particular customer.

When forwarding an invoice question, include the invoice identifier, organisation and period. Do not attach credentials or a full measurement export. A question about a device count can usually be investigated from inventory history without sending measurement contents to the billing contact.
