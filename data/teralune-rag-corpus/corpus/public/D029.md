# Team administration

## Invitations and ownership

A team invitation expires after 48 hours. A workspace permits at most 12 teams. Sending another invitation does not grant temporary access before it is accepted. Review the intended workspace and recipient identity before creating an invitation, especially when a person belongs to several organisations.

A team is a workspace object. Reusing the same visible team name in another workspace does not make the memberships identical. Keep the workspace identifier with the team identifier in an administration log. A screenshot containing only a team name is insufficient evidence that a user was assigned to the correct workspace.

## Changing a role

Team administration requires teams:manage. A role change takes effect on the next authorized request. A previously generated export file is not recalled by changing the role that created it. Downstream copies require their own handling.

An application should recheck access when serving a cached response rather than relying on the role recorded when the response was cached. An administrative view can show the most recent membership state while a separate client still holds an older local display. Treat the service authorization outcome as authoritative for the next request.

## Last administrator safeguard

Removing the last workspace administrator is rejected. Assign another administrator and verify that the assignment succeeded before removing the previous one. A pending invitation does not count as an accepted administrator assignment.

The safeguard protects workspace administration only. It does not make an administrator a member of the internal Operations or Security team. Product roles do not expand access to company runbooks or another customer organisation. Use the appropriate access process for those records.
